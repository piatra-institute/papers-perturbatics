## 2026-07-21 — best-shape pass: scientific honesty and rhythm (v7)

Two independent expert reads commissioned after the v6 fixes, one an adversarial referee for a top interdisciplinary venue, one a literary editor for serious nonfiction, and their findings triaged and adopted. The through-line of the referee read: because the "What Perturbatics is not" section already concedes almost every neighbour, the whole weight of originality rests on the dual map, so that construction must be stated with total precision. Four scientific repairs and a rhythm pass followed. All prior-art locators web-verified before citing.

Scientific. (1) The legibility term is now owned as what it is. By the linearity of the Shapley value, $L_i = \phi^E_i - \phi^C_i = \phi_i(\nu_E - \nu_C)$: the difference of the two maps is the Shapley value of a single net-appearance game, no new operator. The paper now says so, at the definition and in the three-claims paragraph, and relocates the novelty to the pairing of the two games and the reading of their difference, which sharpens the claim rather than shrinking it. (2) The word "legibility" is defended against its established technical sense: in robotics, legible motion is motion shaped so an observer reads the true goal quickly (Dragan, Lee, and Srinivasa, 2013), where legibility serves a real goal; here the term is the residue of that readability when the advertised goal-directedness is not realized. (3) The Shapley-attribution lineage the paper implicitly joins is now cited: the foundational unified account (Lundberg and Lee, 2017), the causal-graph form (Wang, Wiens, and Lundberg, 2021), and the recent turn onto agents, over tools (Horovicz, 2025) and over the steps of a failed run (Shah, 2026); and the closest neighbour to the whole thesis, an interventional causal criterion for agenthood from the same group as the goal-directedness measure already cited (Kenton et al., 2023). (4) The consciousness claim, previously "the mutual information is zero on every observable channel," overclaimed; it is channel- and model-relative. It now concedes the reversible probes (Casali et al., 2013; Butlin et al., 2023) that do return graded readings while a system runs, then lands the narrower and sharper point: the decisive probe is the irreversible ending, uninformative after the fact, forbidden, and for a digital individual unstatable, and the reversible indices carry no calibration for a novel case. Overclaims trimmed: the demonstrator is now stated plainly to carry no empirical content (a worked consistency check, its persona value inserted by the pure-channel construction rather than discovered); the harness "signature of an executor" softened to "consistent with" (two near-zero estimates); the 4-point richness coefficient dropped from prose and figure in favour of the qualitative dissociation; and the abstract's battery sentence disambiguated (the battery separates every pair, no single probe separating all of them) and its rest-0.50 marked as the identity of the paths rather than a failed classifier.

Literary. The rhythm flag lived in the results narration, where quantitative sentences swelled past 80 words with no short one to breathe against. The number paragraphs (lines for the two maps, the boundary sweep, the richness guard, the section opener, the do-Shapley identifiability sentence) were rebroken to let short declaratives land, on the model of the paper's own best passages, without losing an idea or a number. Five negate-then-pivot constructions the house style forbids were removed, including the abstract closer ("what is left is no longer a number; it is a relation"). Mean sentence length fell from 32 words to 27, and the worst airless run, which lived almost entirely in the results, is gone; the longest remaining run is the theoretical core, left dense by choice.

## v10 (2026-08-01) — the centaur instantiated, Simondon, and an interactive agentoscope

**1. Added `analysis_centaur`: a six-component assemblage in which goal authority is a role rather than a part, held by either a human operator or a machine register, with the non-holder left as a pure channel. Machine authority is slaved to the announced target channel, so it re-aims immediately and a decoy moves it; human authority is an operator who knows the mission, so a decoy does not move it and re-aiming is delayed.
