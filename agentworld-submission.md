## Fit (why it answers the call)

The call states its themes as open questions, and this paper answers several of them with a formal method the brief does not itself contain. The brief's own pull-quote, "agentic AI delinks performed subjectivity from functional agency," names a phenomenon the paper turns into a measurement: the legibility term $L = \phi^E - \phi^C$, the gap between the agency a component shows and the agency it realizes. Perturbatics answers, in computable form:

- **Q3** (performance of subjectivity; "whether the performance of subjectivity between ultimately unlike minds is finally irresolvable") — the dual map, the legibility term, and the consciousness boundary. This is the centerpiece.
- **Q2** (agency as ecological + philosophical construct; whether decomposability implies a radicalized variation) — the separation principle and the restricted object.
- **Q4** (provisional individuation vs. accountability to a single agent) — the boundary declared and swept, the do-Shapley attribution of "which component owns the agency."
- **Q9 / Q11** (multiagent simulation as a force; design primitives that presume cooperation and adversariality) — the model-separating probe battery and the mimic/theater detector.

Honest caveat: the paper is a bullseye for the *agency / subjectivity / decomposition* cluster (brief sections 2, 3, 6, 8). It is not about society-scale coevolution or niche construction (sections 1, 5, 10); it reads one assemblage rigorously rather than a multi-agent society. That is a clean, single-contribution fit, not a weakness.

## Proposal (≤500 words, ready to paste)

**Perturbatics: Reading Agency in Assemblages That Cannot Be Read at Rest**

An artificial agent reveals nothing of its agency at rest. It answers as though one mind stood behind its tokens, while what stands there is a composite of a model, a persona, a memory, a harness, permissions, and tools, and the properties worth knowing, whether it pursues a goal, whether it is competent, who authored its output, are the ones its surface cannot carry. This paper gives a method for reading them.

The obstacle is that the theater and the mechanism present the same surface. A model that performs a promise and a model that keeps one emit the same string; a perfectly regulated variable and a merely undisturbed one leave the same flat record. We state a separation principle: when two organizational models agree over the regime a system has been in, no analysis of that record tells them apart, and separation requires a probe, a controlled intervention that makes the rival accounts predict differently. Agency at rest is not faint. It is absent. It has to be perturbed into the record.

From the principle we build an instrument, a centaur agentoscope. Agency is scored as a Bayes factor between a goal model and a passive model. A probe battery, move the goal, block the path, swap the persona, cut the memory, separates systems that are identical when watched. The instrument then attributes the result across the assemblage by two do-Shapley maps, one over the evidence for agency and one over the realized capacity. Their difference is a legibility term, and it makes agency theater a number: a narrating persona shows agency it does not realize, where the goal register realizes what it shows. This answers Agentworld's question of which component owns the apparent agency, and its deeper one, whether the performance of subjectivity between unlike minds is resolvable, in a form one can compute.

The account names its own boundary. For agency the discriminating probe is cheap and repeatable. For consciousness it is not: the decisive probe is the irreversible one, turning the system off, which returns no reading the rival hypotheses can be told apart by, must not be run, and for a copyable digital individual cannot even be stated. So the science must also say when not to perturb, and there the instrument stops, at a stance rather than a measurement, the grief, or its absence, of whoever turned the machine down.

The contribution is a method for one problem class the call opens: the restricted object, organizational predicates of a decomposable agent; the model-separating probe as primitive; the dual realization map with its legibility term; and the boundary treated as a swept variable rather than an assumption. A full 20-page draft, a runnable gridworld simulation, and three figures accompany this proposal.
